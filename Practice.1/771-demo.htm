771 DEMO

A downloadable game for Windows

771  -  is a demo of an indie first-person horror walking simulator.

ABOUT THE TEAM

This game is a small experiment. The project is the result of work by seven developers. Each developer spent one day working on the project, unaware of what the others had done before their turn. 7 developers. 7 days. 1 game!

DESCRIPTION

On an ordinary night, your world changed completely. A familiar apartment leads you to places whose existence is hard to believe. The light, the interior, the walls—everything takes on new forms, drawing you ever deeper into the darkness.

GAMEPLAY


You must leave your cozy bed and visit eerie places. Search for an exit and explore strange corridors leading to yet another location you will long remember.

FEATURES

- Walking simulator.

- Diverse visual styles.

- Realistic graphics.

- High-quality sound.
